## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Social Sharing Links on Posts
The system SHALL provide interactive social sharing links on blog post pages for Twitter (X), Threads, and Facebook. These links SHALL automatically include the current post's URL and title in the shared content.

#### Scenario: Clicking a share button
- **WHEN** a user clicks a share button for a specific platform
- **THEN** a new window or tab SHALL open with the platform's sharing interface, pre-populated with the post's title and absolute URL

### Requirement: Share Button Placement
Share buttons SHALL be placed in a prominent position within the blog post layout (e.g., at the top or bottom of the article) to encourage readers to spread the content.

#### Scenario: Visibility of share buttons
- **WHEN** a user is viewing a blog post
- **THEN** social share buttons SHALL be clearly visible and accessible
